mimir Home Calendar

A private Google Calendar connection for one household's Home Assistant server.

This app connects a family's Google Calendars to Home Assistant running on a server in their own home. It lets the household see upcoming events on its home dashboard and ask the home assistant about the day ahead.

It is not a public product or service. It is operated by one person for the members of one household, and nobody outside that household is invited to use it.

What it does with Google data

With the permission of the person who signs in, it reads their calendars and events, and it can create or change an event when a household member asks Home Assistant to. The data stays on the home server. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. The details are in the privacy policy.
